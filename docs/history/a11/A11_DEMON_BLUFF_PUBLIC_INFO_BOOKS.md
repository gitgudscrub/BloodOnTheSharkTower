# A.11 — Demon Bluff Public-Info Books

Extends the temporary Flowergirl / Town Crier public-information books to Demons
who have those characters as bluffs.

During Night, once the Demon reaches their configured house:
- Flowergirl bluff -> receives the Day's counted YES-voter book.
- Town Crier bluff -> receives the Day's nominator book.
- If both are bluffs, the Demon receives both books.

The books still contain public history only. They never reveal whether a Demon
voted or whether a Minion nominated.

Existing behaviour is preserved:
- Real Flowergirl / Town Crier get their corresponding book.
- Drunk / Marionette believing they are one of those roles get the corresponding book.
- Temporary books are removed at Dawn.
