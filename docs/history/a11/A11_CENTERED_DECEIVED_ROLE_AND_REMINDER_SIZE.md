# A.11 — Centered Drunk/Marionette Pair + Larger Reminder Tokens

- Drunk/Marionette true-role + perceived-role boxes are now laid out as one centred pair.
  The normal player radial position is the centre of the pair, rather than the true role
  staying centred and the perceived role extending only to one side.
- Ordinary reminder tokens are now 16x16 compared with the main 32x32 role token.
  That is one quarter of the main token's area and is much easier to see/click than the
  previous 12x12 markers.
- Reminder lettering is vertically and horizontally centred in the larger token.
