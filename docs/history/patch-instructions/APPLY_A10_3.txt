A.10.3 Grimoire flow/reveal patch

Base: Blood on the Sharktower 1.1.0-dev-a.10.2

Changes:
1. Choosing a player role or Demon bluff returns directly to the Grimoire.
2. Escape/Back from the role chooser returns to the Grimoire.
3. Final Grimoire uses the live Grimoire's radial role/head/seat layout.
4. Final alignment is shown as a blue/red outline around each role token.
5. Hovering a final-reveal player shows their role/alignment/status in the centre.

For a source checkout, overwrite the included files at their matching paths.
