# Storyteller Guide

You run the game: you hand out characters, wake players at night, give them information and keep the Grimoire.

The automation helps, but every call is yours. Press H for keys and help.

# Taking control

Type /bots storyteller claim during setup. Once roles are sent, Storyteller control is locked until the game ends.

- R: the Grimoire.
- I: Storyteller Tools.
- Y: the timer.

# Setting up

The Grimoire's setup checklist walks you through it:

1. Choose a Script: Base 3, a saved script, or paste a botcscripts.com link.
2. Seats: players are seated as they join. To Seats and To Home move everyone.
3. Bag: Fill the Rest picks a fair set of characters.
4. Drunk and Marionette: click the player after the deal.

Then Send Roles.

## Houses

Each seat needs a house: stand in it and use /bots setSeatHome <seat>. A house is the area within 8 blocks of that spot, with its own voice room.

# First night

Press START NIGHT. Night 1 reminders (Red Herring, Washerwoman and others) are in the Grimoire.

The Tonight checklist lists the wake order. The arrow keys step through it and Up visits the highlighted player.

# House visits

With House visits on, a player who chooses at night sleeps until you reach their house. Then they wake and choose.

Their Resolve card also offers:
- Wake now
- Choose seat (choose for them)
- I'll handle it

Turn House visits off in the Grimoire to wake everyone at dusk instead.

# Resolve cards

Automation asks you before anything happens: Poisoner and Monk choices, Imp kills, Slayer shots, Virgin nominations, Witch curses and more.

They wait at the lower right of the Grimoire, with a button for each choice. Nothing is decided without you.

# Night

Visit each awake player, give them their information in their house voice room and place reminders.

Reminders are small tokens on the rim of a character; most expire on their own. At the end of the night, start the day.

# Day

Let the town talk. Open nominations from Tools, Nomination Flow.

- Shift+left click a seat picks the nominator, Shift+right click the nominee.
- Start Vote runs the clock hand.
- Execute Marked executes the player on the block when nominations close.

Then start the next night.

# The Wraith

When the Wraith is in play, they roam at night in Spectator. They hear a house only if they come in by its door.

Every seated player gets a Spirit Lantern to light once per game. A lit lantern warns its owner of a Wraith and names them if they come in by the door.

You are told where the Wraith goes. Recall now and Bring to seat are on their Resolve card.

# Ending the game

When a win happens, a "Game over?" card offers Good wins or Evil wins. You can also end the game from Tools, Game End.

The Grimoire is revealed and the game is sent to the Sharktower site for stats (/bots stats off to skip). Cancel End Game takes it back.

Reset for Next Game puts the town back for the next one.

# Practice players

Rehearse alone, or try a full town: /bots practice add 7 brings in stand-in players around you.

Seat and deal as usual. They walk home at night, come to the square by day, vote, and make night picks after your visit.

/bots practice votes yes, no or random. /bots practice clear sends them away. Practice games are not sent to the site.

# Tips

- Stay a step ahead: check the Tonight checklist before dusk.
- Drunk and poisoned players get false information. Make it believable.
- Keep the game close. A good game is decided at the end.
- Use Undo if you click something by mistake.
